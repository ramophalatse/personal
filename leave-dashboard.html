Note on methodology: Days are counted as working days only (Mon–Fri), excluding South African public holidays visible in the calendar (New Year's Day, Human Rights Day, Good Friday, Family Day, Freedom Day, Workers' Day, Youth Day, National Women's Day, Heritage Day, Day of Reconciliation, Christmas Day, Day of Goodwill). Leave types included: Annual Leave, Flexi-Leave, and Sick Leave. "Flexi" single-day entries are counted as 1 working day each. 2026 figures now cover January–August for all three, read directly from the Team Absence Calendar (own view for Ramo, peer view for TB and NB). Exact day-level markers on the calendar grid are shown for Ramo only, since his own-calendar audit confirmed individual dates; TB and NB's June–August figures are accurate monthly totals from clearly legible date-range blocks (e.g. TB 22–26 Jun, NB 10–14 Aug) but aren't marked day-by-day on the shared grid. Ramo's August figure reflects the confirmed 6–7 Aug break, replacing an earlier-proposed 10–14 Aug week that was not taken. September–December 2026 remain unlogged for TB and NB. For Ramo, September and December show planned (not yet taken) leave in a lighter green, sourced from the 2026 leave plan: 10, 11, 21, 22, 23 & 25 Sep (10–11 Sep added separately, plus the Heritage Day bridge, 6 days total) and 21, 22, 23, 24, 28, 29, 30 & 31 Dec (extended by 21 Dec and 28 Dec, plus the NYC/Miami/Caribbean trip, 8 days total). October and November show no planned leave. Figures and bars marked with an asterisk (*) are planned/projected, not confirmed actuals, and should not be read as logged leave. Note: actual (28) plus planned (14) totals 42 days against the 40-day annual budget — 2 days over.
